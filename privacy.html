Last updated: August 11, 2026

This website and its lead forms are operated by Sam Franco ("we", "me"), based in British Columbia, Canada. This policy explains what happens to information you share with us.

What we collect

  • Contact details you submit through a form — name, email address, phone number.

How we use it

  • To contact you about your inquiry — by phone, email, or text.

What we don't do

  • We do not share it with third parties except the service providers needed to run this site and our scheduling/advertising tools (e.g., Vercel, Calendly, Meta).

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your information or stop contacting you at any time — email me@samfranco.ca and we'll take care of it promptly.
